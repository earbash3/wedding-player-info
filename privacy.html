Privacy Policy

Wedding Player is a private, non-commercial application for one wedding event. It runs on the organiser's Mac and is available only to invited guests on the same local network. The application owner and data controller is Márkus Richárd, who can be contacted at earbash3@gmail.com.

Information processed

Information supplied or generated by guests includes an optional nickname, song search terms, submitted YouTube links, requested videos, queue and priority actions, quiz answers, request times and connection times. Wedding Player also generates a random device identifier and a random device authentication token for each browser. The local server may temporarily process a device's local-network IP address in request logs and for security and rate limiting.

The organiser's browser additionally uses an administrator session and a playback-host session to protect configuration and ensure that only the trusted Mac controls audible playback. Wedding Player does not request a guest's real name, email address, Google Account or YouTube Account.

YouTube API Services

Wedding Player uses YouTube API Services for video search and playlist metadata, and the standard YouTube IFrame Player for playback. By using these features, users also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The YouTube API Data accessed and stored by Wedding Player may include search terms, video and playlist identifiers, video and playlist item titles, channel or video-owner names, thumbnail URLs, video duration, embeddability, live-broadcast status and playlist-item privacy status. This information is used only to display YouTube search results, validate submitted content, populate and operate the shared event queue, show the current and upcoming videos, and start playback in the trusted Mac's embedded player.

Search terms, video identifiers and playlist identifiers are sent to Google only when needed to perform the requested YouTube operation. YouTube API Data and user information are not sold, used for advertising, combined with unrelated data or shared with unrelated third parties. Google and YouTube receive the requests and playback information necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policy.

Wedding Player does not use Google OAuth, does not request access to a user's Google or YouTube account, and does not collect YouTube account credentials or authorization tokens. It does not download, extract, proxy, alter or store YouTube audio or video.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is used only to operate the shared queue, enforce guest limits, moderate requests, restore event state and maintain the reliability and security of the application during the private event. The processing is based on the organiser's legitimate interest in operating the event service and the guest's voluntary use of it.

Browser storage, cookies and third-party content

On guest devices, Wedding Player stores the random device identifier, device authentication token and display-theme preference in browser local storage. On the trusted Mac, it also stores the playback-host session in browser session storage. The local server places an HTTP-only, same-site session cookie in an administrator's browser after a successful PIN login. The administrator cookie and playback-host session are limited to their respective browser sessions; local-storage values remain until the browser data is cleared or the event application is reset.

The embedded YouTube player allows Google and YouTube to provide third-party content, which may include advertisements. Google and YouTube may place, access or recognise cookies or similar technologies and may receive browser, device, network and playback information needed to deliver, secure and measure their services. Wedding Player does not interfere with these technologies. Their use is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Server storage, sharing and retention

Wedding Player stores event data only in the local database on the organiser's Mac. It is not uploaded to a Wedding Player cloud service. The organiser removes the event database when it is no longer needed after the event, except for a limited backup retained only for recovery or legal compliance.

YouTube search results and video metadata caches are checked automatically when the server starts and at least once every 24 hours. Stored YouTube API Data is refreshed or deleted no later than 30 calendar days after it was obtained. Metadata for an active YouTube queue item is refreshed within the same 30-day period. If it cannot be refreshed by the deadline, the item and its API metadata are removed; completed or removed YouTube queue records are also deleted no later than 30 days after their metadata was obtained.

Deletion and user choices

A guest may ask the organiser at earbash3@gmail.com to remove their nickname, pending requests, quiz data or other locally stored data related to them. A valid deletion request will be handled as soon as practical and within seven calendar days. Deleting Wedding Player data does not delete any information held by YouTube or Google.

Because Wedding Player does not use Google OAuth or Authorized Data, it has no Google authorization token to revoke. Users can nevertheless review and revoke authorizations granted to other applications on the Google security settings page. Guests may stop using Wedding Player at any time, close the page and clear the site's browser data to remove locally stored device values.

Security

Administration is PIN-protected, state-changing requests are authorised by the local server, and playback-host capability is restricted to the trusted Mac. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, complaints or deletion requests, contact Márkus Richárd at earbash3@gmail.com.
